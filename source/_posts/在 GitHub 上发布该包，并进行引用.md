一项新的研究调查了在体育活动计划中加入正念训练是否能鼓励大学生保持更健康的锻炼习惯。
The research, conducted across three sites in England, examined how a 30-day digital mindfulness intervention affected students who were previously inactive.
这项研究在英国的三个地点进行，考察了为期 30 天的数字正念干预如何影响之前不活跃的学生。

Physical inactivity is common among university students and can contribute to poor mental health and wellbeing. While physical activity (PA) programs can help, many struggle to keep up long-term behavior changes.
缺乏运动在大学生中很常见，这会导致心理健康和幸福感不佳。虽然体育活动 (PA) 项目可以提供帮助，但许多人仍然难以保持长期的行为改变。
Researchers wanted to see if mindfulness training—designed to build psychological skills and PA-related thinking—could make a difference.
研究人员想看看正念训练（旨在培养心理技能和 PA 相关思维）是否能发挥作用。

The study involved 109 participants who were randomly assigned to one of two groups. One group received an activity monitor and a daily step goal of 8,000 steps (PA-only group).
这项研究涉及 109 名参与者，他们被随机分配到两组中。其中一组配备了活动监测器，并设定了每日 8000 步的目标（仅进行体能训练组）。
